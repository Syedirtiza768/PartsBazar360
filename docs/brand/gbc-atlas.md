# GBC Atlas character assets

Last reviewed: 2026-10-07

Global Business Concern's selected concept is Atlas: a gender-neutral golden eagle with the presence and commercial judgment of a corporate business advisor.

The local reference pack is in `assets/brand/gbc-atlas/`. It includes the selected full-body image, four-view turnaround, hand/wing detail sheet, reusable master prompt, exact generation prompts, and Blender production brief.

The key animation design decision is to give Atlas two five-fingered humanoid hands for gestures and removable props, plus a separate pair of articulated back wings. The wing pair is deliberately stylized and must be rigged independently from the arms. The corporate jacket needs reinforced upper-back openings and movement clearance.

These files are generated PNG modeling references; no 3D model, Blender rig, facial animation, or prop interaction has been built or verified. The generated turnaround is visual guidance with a relaxed low-A stance, not a calibrated blueprint or validated bind pose. The Blender artist must resolve small view differences and verify the production brief's motion acceptance requirements.

See the asset pack's README and `blender-production-brief.md` before subsequent modeling or animation work. The concept pin and palette are working brand assets, not a statement that an official logo system has been approved.
